Subscribe to JAYANAS Bill Tracker

Federal budget, appropriations, legislative and energy-policy information from official sources, with tools to search, follow and compare legislation, alerts each user sets, and a daily email digest. Sold by annual subscription to government offices, companies and organizations.

What a subscription includes

  • Budget and appropriations. Status of all twelve regular appropriations bills, continuing resolutions and combined bills; budget resolutions and budget-process bills; CBO cost estimates and publications; the President's budget documents.
  • Legislation. Every bill and resolution of the current Congress, with status, actions, amendments, committees, cosponsors, Congressional Research Service summaries and every published text version.
  • Version comparison. Any two published versions of a bill side by side, with insertions and deletions marked.
  • Votes. House and Senate roll-call votes with totals, party breakdowns and each member's position.
  • Members of Congress. Profiles of every current member: party, state or district, service, committee and subcommittee assignments, sponsored bills and voting record.
  • Energy and minerals. Energy, offshore, minerals and public-lands legislation; Federal Register rules, proposed rules and notices from Interior, its bureaus, Energy, FERC, NRC and PHMSA; committee activity; your own keywords.
  • Search, watch lists and alerts. Keyword and bill-number search with filters; a personal watch list; alerts on keywords, bills, committees or members by email immediately, daily or weekly.
  • Daily digest. An email each business morning covering the previous day's budget, floor, vote, energy and Federal Register activity.
  • Accounts and support. Individual named accounts; a live virtual onboarding session; email and phone support Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM Central, with a reply within one business day.

JAYANAS Bill Tracker covers official actions, documents and votes the day they are published. It does not include original news reporting.
