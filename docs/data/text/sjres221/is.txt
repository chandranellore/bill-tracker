_______________________________________________________________________

                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
 Providing for congressional disapproval of the agreement for civilian 
nuclear cooperation between the United States and the Kingdom of Saudi 
    Arabia pursuant to section 123 of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That, the Congress does not 
favor the proposed agreement for cooperation transmitted to the 
Congress by the President on September 14, 2026.
