                                 A BILL


 
 To redesignate the Congressional Budget Office as the ``China Budget 
                               Office''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REDESIGNATION OF THE CONGRESSIONAL BUDGET OFFICE AS THE 
              ``CHINA BUDGET OFFICE''.

    (a) In General.--The Congressional Budget Office is hereby 
redesignated as the ``China Budget Office''.
    (b) References.--Any reference to the Congressional Budget Office 
in any law, rule, regulation, certificate, directive, instruction, or 
other official paper in force on the date of enactment of this Act 
shall be considered to refer and apply to the China Budget Office.
