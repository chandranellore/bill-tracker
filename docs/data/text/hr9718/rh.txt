                                 A BILL


 
 To require the Administrator of the Small Business Administration to 
 maintain a website for small business concerns relating to onshoring 
            manufacturing capacity, and for other purposes.


 


    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Reshoring American Manufacturing Act 
of 2026'' or the ``RAM Act''.

SEC. 2. INFORMATION AND RESOURCES FOR SMALL BUSINESS CONCERNS RELATING 
              TO ONSHORING MANUFACTURING CAPACITY.

    (a) Webpage Required.--Not later than 60 days after the date of the 
enactment of this Act, the Administrator of the Small Business 
Administration, acting through the Associate Administrator for the 
Office of Manufacturing and Trade of the Administration, shall 
establish and maintain a webpage containing a list of resources and 
information for small business concerns (as defined under section 3 of 
the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632)) relating to onshoring to the 
United States part or all of the manufacturing capacity of such 
concerns.
    (b) Contents.--The resources and information included on the 
webpage required by subsection (a) shall include--
            (1) contact information for general inquiries, relevant 
        offices of the Small Business Administration, and any partner 
        organizations;
            (2) a free tool designed to help small business concerns 
        identify entities that could assist with onshoring to the 
        United States the manufacturing capacity of such concerns; and
            (3) other information, as determined appropriate by the 
        Associate Administrator for the Office of Manufacturing and 
        Trade of the Administration.
    (c) Updates.--Until the date that is 4 years after the date of the 
enactment of this Act, the Associate Administrator for the Office of 
Manufacturing and Trade annually shall seek input from the Chief 
Counsel for Advocacy of the Office of Advocacy of the Small Business 
Administration for improvements to the webpage and updates to the 
information and resources included on such webpage.
    (d) Study and Report.--Not later than 2 years after the date on 
which the webpage is established, the Administrator of the Small 
Business Administration shall conduct a study and submit to Congress a 
report on the effectiveness of the webpage required under this Act. The 
Administrator shall submit to Congress an updated report annually 
thereafter until the date that is 4 years after the date of the 
enactment of this Act.
    (e) Termination.--This Act and the requirements of this Act shall 
terminate 4 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
