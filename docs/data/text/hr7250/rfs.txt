                                 AN ACT


 
  To reauthorize the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 
                                 2000.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REAUTHORIZATION.

    Section 9 of the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 
2000 (Public Law 106-382; 114 Stat. 1457; 123 Stat. 2856; 128 Stat. 
164; 132 Stat. 2906) is amended, in each of subsections (a)(1) and (b), 
by striking ``2026'' and inserting ``2028''.
