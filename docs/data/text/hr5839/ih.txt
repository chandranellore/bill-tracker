                                 A BILL


 
   To amend title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to include 
 certain supplemental coverage provided to individual health insurance 
                    coverage as an excepted benefit.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Supplemental Benefits for 
Individuals Act of 2025''.

SEC. 2. INCLUDING CERTAIN SUPPLEMENTAL COVERAGE PROVIDED TO INDIVIDUAL 
              HEALTH INSURANCE COVERAGE AS AN EXCEPTED BENEFIT.

    Section 2791(c)(4) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 
300gg-91(c)(4)) is amended by inserting ``or individual health 
insurance coverage'' before the period at the end.
