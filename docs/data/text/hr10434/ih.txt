                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to establish a dedicated 
         trust fund for investments in oil and gas communities.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Empower Energy Exporting Communities 
Act''.

SEC. 2. OIL AND GAS COMMUNITY TRUST FUND.

    (a) In General.--Subchapter A of chapter 98 of the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new section:

``SEC. 9512. OIL AND GAS COMMUNITY TRUST FUND.

    ``(a) In General.--There is established in the Treasury of the 
United States a trust fund to be known as the `Oil and Gas Community 
Trust Fund' consisting of such amounts as may be appropriated to such 
Trust Fund as provided in this section and section 9602(b).
    ``(b) Transfers to Trust Fund.--
            ``(1) In general.--There are hereby appropriated to the Oil 
        and Gas Community Trust Fund amounts equivalent to 3 percent of 
        the net revenues received in the Treasury under section 11 from 
        specified oil or gas companies for taxable years beginning 
        after the date of the enactment of this section.
            ``(2) Specified oil or gas company.--The term `specified 
        oil or gas company' means any corporation if a significant 
        portion of the gross receipts of such corporation are 
        attributable to the production, refining, processing, 
        transportation, or distribution of oil, gas, or any primary 
        product thereof.
    ``(c) Expenditures From Trust Fund.--
            ``(1) In general.--Amounts in the Oil and Gas Community 
        Trust Fund shall be available to the Secretary, without need of 
        further appropriation and without fiscal year limitation, for 
        purposes of making payments to eligible oil and gas communities 
        for infrastructure and public service investments.
            ``(2) Eligible oil and gas community.--For purposes of this 
        subsection, the term `eligible oil and gas community' means any 
        county, parish, municipality, or Tribal community if the 
        Secretary determines that such county, parish, municipality, or 
        Tribal community is located within 50 miles of a major port for 
        the export of oil, gas, or any primary product thereof.
            ``(3) Limitation based on export share.--
                    ``(A) In general.--For any fiscal year, payments 
                made under paragraph (1) during such year to eligible 
                oil and gas communities in a port region shall not 
                exceed, in the aggregate, the amount which bears the 
                same ratio to the total amounts available in the Oil 
                and Gas Community Trust Fund as the port region's total 
                amount of exports of oil, gas, or any primary product 
                thereof bears to the total amount of such exports of 
                all major ports nationwide (as determined by the 
                Secretary based on the most recent year for which data 
                is available).
                    ``(B) Port region.--For purposes of this paragraph, 
                the term `port region' means any region within 50 miles 
                of a major port for the export of oil, gas, or any 
                primary product thereof.''.
    (b) Clerical Amendment.--The table of sections for subchapter A of 
chapter 98 of such Code is amended by adding at the end the following 
new item:

``Sec. 9512. Oil and Gas Community Trust Fund.''.
