                                 AN ACT


 
 To amend title 38, United States Code, to update certain terminology 
                     regarding veteran employment.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Veterans Employment Readiness Yield 
Act of 2025'' or the ``VERY Act of 2025''.

SEC. 2. UPDATE OF CERTAIN TERMINOLOGY REGARDING VETERAN EMPLOYMENT.

    Title 38, United States Code, is amended--
            (1) by striking ``employment handicap'' each place it 
        occurs and inserting ``employment barrier''; and
            (2) by striking ``employment handicaps'' each place it 
        occurs and inserting ``employment barriers''.

            Passed the House of Representatives July 21, 2025.
