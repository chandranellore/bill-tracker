                                 A BILL


 
   To extend the authority for modifications to the Second Division 
                 Memorial in the District of Columbia.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. EXTENSION OF AUTHORITY FOR MODIFICATIONS TO SECOND DIVISION 
              MEMORIAL.

    Notwithstanding section 8903(e) of title 40, United States Code, 
the authority provided by section 352 of the National Defense 
Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2018 (Public Law 115-91; 131 Stat. 
1367) shall continue to apply through September 30, 2032.
