                                 A BILL


 
 To reauthorize the Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program 
                              Act of 1994.


 


    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REAUTHORIZATION OF JUNIOR DUCK STAMP CONSERVATION AND DESIGN 
              PROGRAM ACT OF 1994.

    (a) Definition of State.--Section 5 of the Junior Duck Stamp 
Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994 (16 U.S.C. 719b-1) is 
amended--
            (1) by inserting ``and'' after ``Guam,''; and
            (2) by striking ``, and any other territory or possession'' 
        after ``Virgin Islands''.
    (b) Authorization of Appropriations.--Section 6 of the Junior Duck 
Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994 (16 U.S.C. 719c) is 
amended in subsection (a) by striking ``2006 through 2010'' and 
inserting ``2025 through 2031''.
