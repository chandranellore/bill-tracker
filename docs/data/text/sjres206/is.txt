                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
    United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
    Protection Agency relating to ``California State Motor Vehicle 
 Pollution Control Standards; Notice of Decision Granting a Waiver of 
  Clean Air Act Preemption for California's 2009 and Subsequent Model 
    Year Greenhouse Gas Emission Standards for New Motor Vehicles''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to 
``California State Motor Vehicle Pollution Control Standards; Notice of 
Decision Granting a Waiver of Clean Air Act Preemption for California's 
2009 and Subsequent Model Year Greenhouse Gas Emission Standards for 
New Motor Vehicles'' (74 Fed. Reg. 32744 (July 8, 2009)), and such rule 
shall have no force or effect.
