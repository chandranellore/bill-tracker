                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
   Disapproving the proposed civilian nuclear cooperation agreement 
between the United States and Saudi Arabia submitted to the Congress on 
                          September 14, 2026.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves of 
the proposed civilian nuclear cooperation agreement between the United 
States and Saudi Arabia submitted pursuant to section 123 of the Atomic 
Energy Act of 1954 to the Congress on September 14, 2026.
