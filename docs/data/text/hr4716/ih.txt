                                 A BILL


 
  To transfer administrative jurisdiction of certain Federal land in 
   Saratoga Springs, Utah, from the Secretary of the Interior to the 
United States Postal Service for construction of a post office, and for 
                            other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. TRANSFER OF ADMINISTRATIVE JURISDICTION OF LAND TO USPS; 
              CONSTRUCTION OF POST OFFICE.

    (a) Transfer.--
            (1) In general.--Administrative jurisdiction of the Federal 
        land is hereby transferred from the Secretary of the Interior 
        to the United States Postal Service for use as a United States 
        Postal facility, consistent with subsection (b).
            (2) Federal land.--For the purposes of this section, the 
        term ``Federal land'' means the approximately 20.32 acres of 
        Federal land in Saratoga Springs, Utah, known as Parcel 
        #58:022:0021, located approximately west of Mountain View 
        Corridor and north of SR-73 with access off Wild Blossom 
        Boulevard.
    (b) Construction of Post Office.--Not later than 2 years after the 
date of the enactment of this Act, the Postal Service shall construct 
and begin operation of a post office on the Federal land transferred to 
the Postal Service under subsection (a).
