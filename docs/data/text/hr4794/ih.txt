                                 A BILL


 
 To authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to enter into a lease 
                for a Vet Center in Mankato, Minnesota.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. AUTHORITY OF SECRETARY OF VETERANS AFFAIRS TO ENTER INTO A 
              LEASE FOR A VET CENTER IN MANKATO, MINNESOTA.

    Subject to the availability of appropriations for such purpose, the 
Secretary of Veterans Affairs may obligate or expend not more than 
$1,400,000 during fiscal years 2025 through 2026 to enter into a lease 
for a Vet Center (as defined in section 1712A of title 38, United 
States Code) in Mankato, Minnesota.
