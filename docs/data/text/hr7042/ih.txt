                                 A BILL


 
To expand the Weatherization Assistance Program for activities related 
to active duty and reserve military households, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Heroes Home Energy Savings Act''.

SEC. 2. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR WAP ENHANCEMENT AND INNOVATION.

    (a) Funding.--Section 414D of the Energy Conservation and 
Production Act (42 U.S.C. 6864d) is amended--
            (1) in subsection (j)(1), by adding at the end the 
        following:
                    ``(C) Fiscal years 2026 through 2030.--For each of 
                fiscal years 2026 through 2030, of the amount made 
                available under section 422(3) for such fiscal year to 
                carry out the weatherization program under this part 
                (not including any of such amount made available for 
                Department of Energy headquarters training or technical 
                assistance)--
                            ``(i) not more than 6 percent of such 
                        amount made available under section 422(3)(A) 
                        may be used to carry out this section; and
                            ``(ii) $2,100,000 made available under 
                        section 422(3)(B) shall be used to carry out 
                        this section with respect to active duty and 
                        reserve military households.''; and
            (2) in subsection (k), by striking ``2025'' and inserting 
        ``2030''.
    (b) Authorization of Appropriations.--Section 422 of the Energy 
Conservation and Production Act (42 U.S.C. 6872) is amended--
            (1) in paragraph (1), by striking ``and'' at the end;
            (2) in paragraph (2), by striking the period at the end and 
        inserting ``; and''; and
            (3) by adding at the end the following:
            ``(3) for each of fiscal years 2026 through 2030--
                    ``(A) $350,000,000; and
                    ``(B) $2,100,000 to be used to carry out section 
                414D with respect to active duty and reserve military 
                households, as described in section 
                414D(j)(1)(C)(ii).''.
