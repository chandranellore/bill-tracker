                                 A BILL


 
   To direct the Secretary of Energy to establish a grant program to 
  facilitate the deployment of plug-in devices for the generation of 
                  solar power, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Plug-In Solar Savings Act of 2026''.

SEC. 2. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY GRANT PROGRAM FOR DEPLOYMENT OF PLUG-IN 
              SOLAR POWER GENERATION DEVICES.

    (a) Establishment.--Not later than 180 days after the date of 
enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Energy shall establish a grant 
program (in this section referred to as the ``Program'') to facilitate 
the deployment of plug-in solar generation devices.
    (b) Grant Authority; Eligible Recipients.--In carrying out the 
Program, the Secretary may award a grant on a competitive basis to any 
of the following:
            (1) A unit of State or local government.
            (2) A nonprofit organization.
            (3) A public housing agency (as such term is defined in 
        section 3(b) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 
        U.S.C. 1437a(b)).
            (4) A multifamily dwelling that has entered into a 
        partnership with an entity listed in paragraphs (1) through (3) 
        for the purpose of carrying out a project specified in 
        subsection (d).
    (c) Limitations on Grant Amount.--
            (1) Annual cap.--A grant awarded under the Program to an 
        eligible entity may not exceed $2,000,000 per year.
            (2) Administrative cost cap.--A recipient of a grant under 
        the Program may not use more than 15 percent of such grant 
        amounts to cover administrative costs relating to the 
        management of such grant or activities carried out using such 
        grant amounts.
    (d) Eligible Uses of Grant Funds.--A recipient of a grant under the 
Program may only use grant funds for the purpose of carrying out a 
project to--
            (1) distribute plug-in solar generation devices to not 
        fewer than 5 eligible households;
            (2) provide to eligible households technical assistance 
        regarding such devices;
            (3) distribute to eligible households educational materials 
        regarding the benefits of such devices; or
            (4) provide funding to eligible households for home updates 
        to enhance safety with respect to the use of such devices.
    (e) Conditions for Certain Projects.--
            (1) Maximizing household savings.--A recipient of a grant 
        under the Program carrying out a project specified in 
        subsection (d)(1) shall, as a condition of such grant, seek to 
        select, and distribute to eligible households, plug-in solar 
        generation devices in a manner that maximizes household 
        electricity cost savings and, to the extent practicable, 
        provides such eligible households with the ability to monitor 
        the generation of electricity by such devices.
            (2) Consumer information.--A recipient of a grant under the 
        Program carrying out a project specified in subsection (d)(1) 
        shall, as a condition of such grant, provide to each eligible 
        household to which the recipient distributes a plug-in solar 
        generation device basic information regarding--
                    (A) estimated electricity cost savings to result 
                from the use of such device, and factors affecting such 
                estimated cost savings;
                    (B) the safe operation of such device, including 
                relevant electrical safety requirements; and
                    (C) applicable warranty and product recall 
                procedures, and insurance and liability considerations, 
                relating to such device.
            (3) Pass-through of savings.--A recipient of a grant under 
        the Program carrying out any project under subsection (d) with 
        respect to a household residing in a building with multiple 
        dwelling units that is served by a master meter shall, as a 
        condition of such grant, agree to enter into a written contract 
        with the owner of such building to ensure that any electricity 
        cost savings realized as a result of such project are passed 
        through to such household.
    (f) Applications.--To be eligible for a grant under the Program, an 
entity shall submit to the Secretary an application at such time, in 
such form, and containing such information as the Secretary determines 
appropriate.
    (g) Priority.--In awarding grants under the Program, the Secretary 
shall give priority to applicants the proposed projects of which would 
serve eligible households that face barriers to accessing conventional 
rooftop devices for the generation of solar power, including eligible 
households that are tenants and eligible households that are residing 
in buildings with multiple dwelling units.
    (h) Reports to Congress.--
            (1) In general.--On an annual basis, the Secretary shall 
        submit to the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of 
        Representatives and the Committee on Energy and Natural 
        Resources of the Senate a report containing an assessment of 
        the implementation of the Program during the preceding calendar 
        year.
            (2) Matters.--Each report submitted under paragraph (1) 
        shall include, with respect to the period covered by such 
        report, an identification of the following:
                    (A) The number of eligible households that received 
                plug-in solar generation devices under the Program.
                    (B) The status of the residents of such households 
                as renters or homeowners.
                    (C) The number and cost of plug-in solar generation 
                devices distributed under the Program.
                    (D) The aggregate rated power capacity of plug-in 
                solar generation devices distributed under the Program 
                in total, and a breakdown of the rated power capacity 
                of plug-in solar generation devices distributed by each 
                recipient of a grant under the Program.
                    (E) The estimated dollar amount of household 
                electricity cost savings realized as a result of the 
                Program, as determined by the Secretary.
    (i) Authorization of Appropriations.--There is authorized to be 
appropriated to the Secretary to carry out this section $25,000,000 for 
each of fiscal years 2028 through 2030.
    (j) Definitions.--In this section:
            (1) Eligible household.--The term ``eligible household'' 
        means any household--
                    (A) with an income of 80 percent or less than the 
                area median income; or
                    (B) that participates in or is enrolled under, or 
                that includes an individual who participates in or is 
                enrolled under--
                            (i) the supplemental nutrition assistance 
                        program under the Food and Nutrition Act of 
                        2008 (7 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.);
                            (ii) the special supplemental nutrition 
                        program for women, infants, and children 
                        established by section 17 of the Child 
                        Nutrition Act of 1966 (42 U.S.C. 1786);
                            (iii) the low-income home energy assistance 
                        program established under the Low-Income Home 
                        Energy Assistance Act of 1981 (42 U.S.C. 8621 
                        et seq.);
                            (iv) the Weatherization Assistance Program 
                        for Low-Income Persons established under part A 
                        of title IV of the Energy Conservation and 
                        Production Act (42 U.S.C. 6861 et seq.);
                            (v) a State plan (or waiver of such plan) 
                        under title XIX of the Social Security Act (42 
                        U.S.C. 1396 et seq.);
                            (vi) the public housing program under the 
                        United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 
                        1437 et seq.);
                            (vii) the program for rental assistance 
                        under section 8 of the United States Housing 
                        Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437f);
                            (viii) the programs under title IV of the 
                        McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act (42 
                        U.S.C. 11360 et seq.);
                            (ix) the program for supportive housing for 
                        the elderly under section 202 of the Housing 
                        Act of 1959 (12 U.S.C. 1701q);
                            (x) the program for supportive housing for 
                        persons with disabilities under section 811 of 
                        the Cranston-Gonzalez National Affordable 
                        Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 8013);
                            (xi) the AIDS Housing Opportunities program 
                        under subtitle D of title VIII of the Cranston-
                        Gonzalez National Affordable Housing Act (42 
                        U.S.C. 12901 et seq.);
                            (xii) the program for Native American 
                        housing under the Native American Housing 
                        Assistance and Self-Determination Act of 1996 
                        (25 U.S.C. 4101 et seq.);
                            (xiii) the program for housing assistance 
                        for Native Hawaiians under title VIII of the 
                        Native American Housing Assistance and Self-
                        Determination Act of 1996 (25 U.S.C. 4221 et 
                        seq.);
                            (xiv) the programs for assistance for rural 
                        rental housing under title V of the Housing Act 
                        of 1949 (42 U.S.C. 1471 et seq.); or
                            (xv) any other relevant Federal assistance 
                        program, as determined by the Secretary.
            (2) Multifamily dwelling.--The term ``multifamily 
        dwelling'' means a unit of housing that is located within a 
        building consisting of 5 or more units, including an apartment 
        building, a subsidized housing building, an age restricted 
        building, an assisted living dwelling, (such as age restricted 
        building or an assisted living building), and student housing.
            (3) Plug-in solar generation device.--The term ``plug-in 
        solar generation device'' means a device, or combination of 
        devices, for photovoltaic energy generation that--
                    (A) has a maximum aggregated AC output of not more 
                than 1,200 watts per dwelling;
                    (B) is designed to be connected to, and 
                disconnected from, the electrical system of a building 
                through a receptacle;
                    (C) is intended to offset the onsite electricity 
                consumption of the electric consumer concerned;
                    (D) meets the standards of the most recent version 
                of the National Electrical Code;
                    (E) is certified by Underwriters Laboratories or an 
                equivalent nationally recognized testing laboratory; 
                and
                    (F) includes a feature, certified by Underwriters 
                Laboratories or an equivalent nationally recognized 
                testing laboratory, that isolates the device from the 
                electrical system of the building to which such device 
                is connected, to prevent the device from backfeeding 
                electricity to the electric grid in the event of a 
                power outage.
            (4) Secretary.--The term ``Secretary'' means the Secretary 
        of Energy.
            (5) State.--The term ``State'' means each State of the 
        United States, the District of Columbia, each territory or 
        possession of the United States, and each federally recognized 
        Indian Tribe.
