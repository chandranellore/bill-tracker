                                 AN ACT


 
To amend title 5, United States Code, to authorize the increase of the 
          retirement age in the United States Capitol Police.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. INCREASE IN RETIREMENT AGE OF UNITED STATES CAPITOL POLICE 
              OFFICERS.

    (a) Civil Service Retirement System.--Section 8335(c) of title 5, 
United States Code, is amended by striking ``becomes 60 years of age'' 
and inserting ``attains an age determined by the Board that shall be 
not less than 57 years of age and not more than 65 years of age''.
    (b) Federal Employees' Retirement System.--Section 8425(c) of title 
5, United States Code, is amended by striking ``becomes 60 years of 
age'' and inserting ``attains an age determined by the Board that shall 
be not less than 57 years of age and not more than 65 years of age''.

            Passed the House of Representatives April 27, 2026.
