                                 A BILL


 
   To extend the authorization of the African American Civil Rights 
                                Network.


 


    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Renewing the African American Civil 
Rights Network Act''.

SEC. 2. EXTENSION OF AUTHORIZATION.

    Section 308404 of title 54, United States Code, is amended by 
striking ``7 years after the date of enactment of this chapter'' and 
inserting ``7 years after the date of the enactment of the Renewing the 
African American Civil Rights Network Act''.
