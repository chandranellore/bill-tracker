                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
    United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
   Protection Agency relating to ``Phasedown of Hydrofluorocarbons: 
  Management of Certain Hydrofluorocarbons and Substitutes Under the 
          American Innovation and Manufacturing Act of 2020''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to 
``Phasedown of Hydrofluorocarbons: Management of Certain 
Hydrofluorocarbons and Substitutes Under the American Innovation and 
Manufacturing Act of 2020'' (89 Fed. Reg. 82682 (October 11, 2024)), 
and such rule shall have no force or effect.
