                                 A BILL


 
To exempt Medicare from any sequestration under Statutory PAYGO that is 
               caused by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. EXEMPTION OF MEDICARE FROM STATUTORY PAYGO SEQUESTRATION 
              CAUSED BY THE ONE BIG BEAUTIFUL BILL ACT.

    The Medicare programs under title XVIII of the Social Security Act 
(42 U.S.C. 1395 et seq.) shall be exempt from reduction under any 
sequestration order issued on or after the date of enactment of this 
Act under section 5 of the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (2 
U.S.C. 934) that is attributable, in whole or in part, to the budgetary 
effects of the Act entitled ``An Act to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to title II of H. Con. Res. 14'', approved July 4, 2025 
(Public Law 119-21; 139 Stat. 72) (commonly known as the ``One Big 
Beautiful Bill Act'').
