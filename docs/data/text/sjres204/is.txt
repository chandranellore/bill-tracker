                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and 
       Wildlife Service and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
Administration relating to ``Rescinding the Definition of `Harm' Under 
                     the Endangered Species Act''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service and the 
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration relating to 
``Rescinding the Definition of `Harm' Under the Endangered Species 
Act'' (91 Fed. Reg. 43300 (July 14, 2026)), and such rule shall have no 
force or effect.
