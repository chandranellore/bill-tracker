                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
    United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
    Protection Agency relating to ``Emissions Budget and Allowance 
  Allocations for Indiana Under the Revised Cross-State Air Pollution 
                             Rule Update''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to 
``Emissions Budget and Allowance Allocations for Indiana Under the 
Revised Cross-State Air Pollution Rule Update'' (90 Fed. Reg. 21423 
(May 20, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
