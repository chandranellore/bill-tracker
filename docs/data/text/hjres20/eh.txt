                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
 United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy 
    relating to ``Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation 
    Standards for Consumer Gas-fired Instantaneous Water Heaters''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, 


              

 That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of 
Energy relating to ``Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation 
Standards for Consumer Gas-fired Instantaneous Water Heaters'' (89 Fed. 
Reg. 105188 (December 26, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or 
effect.

            Passed the House of Representatives February 27, 2025.

            Attest:

                                                                 Clerk.
