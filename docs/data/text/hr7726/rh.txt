                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Child Care and Development Block Grant Act of 1990 to 
        withhold funds from noncompliant States under such Act.


 


    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``No Funds for Repeat Child Care 
Violations Act of 2026''.

SEC. 2. STRENGTHENING THE AUTHORITY TO WITHHOLD FUNDS FOR FRAUD.

    Section 658I(b)(2)(B) of the Child Care and Development Block Grant 
Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 9858g(b)(2)) is amended by striking ``Secretary 
may'' and inserting ``Secretary shall''.
