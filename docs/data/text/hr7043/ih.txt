                                 A BILL


 
To amend the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act to reauthorize the 
                   transmission facilitation program.

United States of America in Congress assembled,

    Section 40106(d)(3) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act 
(42 U.S.C. 18713(d)(3)) is amended by striking ``2022 through 2026'' 
and inserting ``2026 through 2031''.
