                                 A BILL


 
  To codify Secretary's Order 3434 of the Department of the Interior.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CODIFICATION OF DOI SECRETARY'S ORDER 3434.

    Secretary's Order 3434 issued by the Secretary of the Interior and 
dated June 25, 2025 (relating to strengthening coordination with 
gateway communities), shall have the force and effect of law.
