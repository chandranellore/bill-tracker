                                 A BILL


 
    To amend the Clean Air Act to prohibit the Administrator of the 
     Environmental Protection Agency from requiring the continuous 
 monitoring of the net heating value or related parameters of certain 
          combustion control devices, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Natural Gas Dominance Act''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON REQUIRING CONTINUOUS NHV MONITORING.

    (a) Standards of Performance for New Stationary Sources.--Section 
111 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7411) is amended by adding at the 
end the following:
    ``(k) Crude Oil and Natural Gas Facilities.--
            ``(1) Prohibition on requiring continuous nhv monitoring.--
        In carrying out this section, the Administrator may not require 
        the owner or operator of a crude oil and natural gas facility 
        to continuously monitor the net heating value (or a related 
        parameter) of a combustion control device, including a flare 
        and enclosed combustion device.
            ``(2) Net heating value defined.--In this subsection, the 
        term `net heating value' means NHV<INF>cz</INF> or 
        NHV<INF>dil</INF> (as each such term is defined in section 
        63.670(m) of title 40, Code of Federal Regulations (as in 
        effect on the date of enactment of this subsection)).''.
    (b) Hazardous Air Pollutants.--Section 112(n)(4) of the Clean Air 
Act (42 U.S.C. 7412(n)(4)) is amended by adding at the end the 
following:
                    ``(C) Prohibition on requiring continuous nhv 
                monitoring.--
                            ``(i) In general.--In carrying out this 
                        section, the Administrator may not require the 
                        owner or operator of an oil and gas exploration 
                        or production well or a natural gas 
                        transmission and storage facility to 
                        continuously monitor the net heating value (or 
                        a related parameter) of a combustion control 
                        device, including a flare and enclosed 
                        combustion device.
                            ``(ii) Net heating value defined.--In this 
                        subsection, the term `net heating value' means 
                        NHV<INF>cz</INF> or NHV<INF>dil</INF> (as each 
                        such term is defined in section 63.670(m) of 
                        title 40, Code of Federal Regulations (as in 
                        effect on the date of enactment of this 
                        subparagraph)).''.

SEC. 3. DURATION OF ROUTING TO FLARES OR CONTROL DEVICES BY CERTAIN 
              WELLS.

    (a) Prohibition on Reducing Maximum Allowed Duration During 
Malfunctions or Safety Incidents.--The Administrator of the 
Environmental Protection Agency may not promulgate a rule that reduces, 
to a number of hours below 72 hours, the maximum number of hours that 
an owner or operator of a well that is complying with paragraph (a) of 
section 60.5391c, or paragraph (a) of section 60.5377b, of title 40, 
Code of Federal Regulations (or any successor regulations), may 
temporarily route associated gas to a flare or control device during a 
malfunction or incident that endangers the safety of operator personnel 
or the public.
    (b) Allowed Duration During Service Interruptions.--The owner or 
operator of a well complying with paragraph (a)(1) of section 60.5391c, 
or paragraph (a)(1) of section 60.5377b, of title 40, Code of Federal 
Regulations (or any successor regulations), may route associated gas to 
a flare or control device for the duration of a temporary interruption 
in service from the gathering or pipeline system, or an amount of time 
that the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency 
determines to be necessary that is no less than 90 days, whichever is 
less.
