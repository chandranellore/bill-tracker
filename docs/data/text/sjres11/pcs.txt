                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Ocean Energy 
     Management relating to ``Protection of Marine Archaeological 
                              Resources''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Bureau of Ocean Energy Management relating to 
``Protection of Marine Archaeological Resources'' (89 Fed. Reg. 71160 
(September 3, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
