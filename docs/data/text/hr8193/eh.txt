                                 AN ACT


 
 To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located 
   at 189 East Main Street in Xenia, Ohio, as the ``Gil Whitney Post 
                           Office Building''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. GIL WHITNEY POST OFFICE BUILDING.

    (a) Designation.--The facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 189 East Main Street in Xenia, Ohio, shall be known and 
designated as the ``Gil Whitney Post Office Building''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to 
in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Gil 
Whitney Post Office Building''.

            Passed the House of Representatives September 16, 2026.
