                                 A BILL


 
  To amend title 49, United States Code, to include aircraft deicing 
 storage facilities in the definition of airport development, and for 
                            other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Facility for Runway Operations and 
Safe Transportation Act'' or the ``FROST Act''.

SEC. 2. DEICING EQUIPMENT PROTECTION.

    Section 47102 of title 49, United States Code, is amended--
            (1) in paragraph (3)(B)(v) by striking ``and storage 
        facilities for the equipment and fluids''; and
            (2) in paragraph (24) by inserting ``storage facilities for 
        aircraft deicing equipment and fluids,'' after ``sponsor,''.
