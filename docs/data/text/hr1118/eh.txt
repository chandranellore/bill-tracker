                                 AN ACT


 
 To amend title 41, United States Code, to provide best value through 
      the multiple award schedule program, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Value Over Cost Act of 2026''.

SEC. 2. PROVIDING BEST VALUE THROUGH THE MULTIPLE AWARD SCHEDULE 
              PROGRAM.

    Section 152(3)(B) of title 41, United States Code, is amended by 
striking ``lowest overall cost alternative'' and inserting ``best 
value''.

            Passed the House of Representatives July 21, 2026.
