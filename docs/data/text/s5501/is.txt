                                 A BILL


 
To amend the Alaska Power Administration Asset Sale and Termination Act 
  to modify and continue an exemption from application of the Federal 
   Power Act for the Snettisham Hydroelectric Project, and for other 
                               purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. MODIFICATION AND CONTINUATION OF EXEMPTION.

    Section 104(b) of the Alaska Power Administration Asset Sale and 
Termination Act (Public Law 104-58; 42 U.S.C. 7152 note) is amended--
            (1) by striking ``or Snettisham''; and
            (2) by striking ``or the Snettisham Purchaser''.
